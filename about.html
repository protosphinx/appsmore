About appsmore

Remember Ninite, before the installer? It was a plain page with the handful of Windows programs worth having, sorted into a few columns. You ticked what you wanted and got on with your day. No charts, no "trending", no ads dressed up as recommendations.

appsmore is that for the iPhone. One person's list, kept short on purpose.

How the list is chosen

By hand. An app is on here because it is good at its job, respects you, and has stayed that way. It comes off the list when that stops being true. Nothing is paid for, nothing is generated.

Why it can't "install everything at once"

iOS only installs apps through the App Store. So the link you get is a checklist: each Get button drops you into the store page for that app, you tap install, swipe back, and take the next one. Your progress is remembered on the phone.

Privacy

The whole list lives in the link itself. There is no account, no database, and no tracking. Icons are loaded from Apple's servers.
