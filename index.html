The good iPhone apps. Hand-picked.

No top-100 charts, no sponsored junk. A short list of apps that are actually worth a home-screen slot. Tick the ones you want and get one link for your phone. Handy when you're setting up a fresh iPhone, the new iPhone Duo included.

How it works

  1. Pick apps below.
  2. Get a link (or scan the QR code).
  3. Open it on your iPhone and tap Get next to each one - it drops you straight into the App Store.
